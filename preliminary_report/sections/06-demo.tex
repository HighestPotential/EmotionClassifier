\section{Demo Implementation}
\label{sec:demo}

To demonstrate the practical application of our work, we will implement a real-time video pipeline using \textbf{OpenCV}, adapting the approach described by Kumar~\cite{kumar2018demonstration}.

The planned system consists of two main steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Face Detection:} We will use OpenCV's Haar cascade classifiers to scan the video frame. This algorithm identifies the coordinates of the face, allowing us to isolate the facial region from the background.
    \item \textbf{Classification:} The detected face region will be cropped and resized to $64 \times 64$ pixels. This processed image is then passed to our model to classify the emotion.
\end{enumerate}

The final output will display the video feed with a bounding box and the emotion label overlaid in real-time.
