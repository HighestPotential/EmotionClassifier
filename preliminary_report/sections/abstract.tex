\begin{abstract}
    In this report we share our approach to the facial emotion 
    recognition problem. We identified a number of fitting datasets 
    that were well documented in literature and have already been shown
    to produce good results. Although finding high quality datasets is 
    essential, preprocessing said data is just as important for high 
    quality emotion classification Models. This step is done by comparing 
    all the collected samples and removing duplicates, unusable images where 
    the subject is not clearly recognizable or mislabeled data. Finally the 
    model architecture will be determined after evaluating the accuracy of 
    different, self implemented designs.
\end{abstract}
